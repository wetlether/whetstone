## What it is
A personal trainer for your *coding judgment*, built straight out of your blind grading. The core loop is your one elite skill — **you're the discriminator**: it shows you two real moves, you pick the better, it shows you why, and it tracks you over time.

## Two modes (the new part you asked for)
- **🪨 Train your weak spots** — fix the gaps. Pick the better of two moves, then pick the *precise reason* it's better, from four near-identical reasons (trains the one thing you're weakest at: spelling out the why).
- **✨ Exploit your strengths** — given three approaches to a situation, pick the one **that's yours to make** — the one that plays to your verified talents. The answer never names a skill (that'd give it away); a **🎯 My verified skill set** button is always there if you blank.

## The bar it's built to (this is the important part)
You're an elite test-taker — you read what a question *wants* you to say. So every item is **mirror-form**: both options equal length, equal tone, no virtue-words, sometimes the "responsible-sounding" one is wrong. Then a **cue-attacker** that may only read the *shape* (not the substance) has to score at **chance** or the item is **cut**. It cut **~70% of my own first drafts**, including 8 I thought were clean. What survives can't be gamed by reading it — only by knowing the truth.

## Also in there
- **"I disagree — bank it"** on every answer → you type your different read → it pins to a **Banked thoughts** page you can bring to me for the real conversation. Plus **"judgment call"** items with no wrong answer, where you just make your case.
- A **mastery spiral** — each skill climbs warm-up → for real → the fine ear → master as you prove it.
